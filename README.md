### Project group 7 Autonomous economy and taxation


Simulations of a human/AI mixed economy and how taxation might work in this economy.

## Introduction

Project has three agents, the firm, the household and the government. Depending on how demand evolves,
the firm hires people from the households and spin up new AI agents. Money is paid to the human workers,
and with this money they pay taxes and buy stuff created by the firm. The humans are therefore driving demand.

The government has a budget depending on tax income and over a certain threshold, it starts giving back money to the households.
This money is a representation of welfare and with this money the households by stuff from the firm and drives demand.

The AI workers produces but don't consume and can therefore not drive demand.
